[h1]TTT Bots Revamped[/h1]

A maintained, heavily bug-fixed and expanded build of TTT Bots 2 - the bot addon for Trouble in Terrorist Town.
Built for TTT2, and still fully playable in plain TTT.

[b]This is an unofficial revamp of the original TTT Bots 2, not the original addon.[/b] It is the same project
under the same licence (CC BY-SA 4.0); everything below is what has changed.

[h2]More TTT2 roles are actually played properly[/h2]
15 roles the original had no behaviour for now play with their own rules instead of a generic stand-in:
Psychopath, Bandit, Gambler, Doctor, Roider, Fuse, Mesmerist, Thrall, Beggar, Collusionist, Blocker, Cursed,
Revenant, Pharaoh and Graverobber. For example: the Roider only fights with its crowbar, the Beggar and the
Collusionist never take a target because they cannot hurt anybody, the Mesmerist's defibrillator revives anyone,
the Cursed leaves its role by swapping with another player, and the Revenant changes sides when it dies.

[h2]Bots use the items their role is given[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ankh (Pharaoh / Graverobber): place it, hold the conversion, carry it somewhere safer, or shoot an enemy's down.
[*]Grenades: pin pulled and thrown the way the weapon works, and only at an enemy they have lost sight of.
[*]Super Soda cans, plus the Fake Soda decoy left where a real can belongs.
[*]Boom Body, the Thomas the Tank Engine gun and the Minethrower when a bot has bought one.
[*]Defibrillators, held for as long as the weapon asks for, and health stations.
[/list]

[h2]Fights that make sense[/h2]
[list]
[*]Traitors no longer open fire the instant a round begins (ttt_bot_attack_delay).
[*]An unprovoked kill waits too (ttt_bot_rdm_delay).
[*]Melee bots close to their weapon's real reach instead of swinging at air.
[*]In the post-round deathmatch, roles that cannot deal damage hide instead of walking into a fight they cannot win.
[*]A sniper perch is only used by a bot carrying a weapon that can actually use one.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes and performance[/h2]
Vision no longer depends on an NPC-only function that reports "cannot see" for every player on a server with
[b]ai_ignoreplayers[/b] set - on such a server the original build's bots were effectively blind. Also fixed: bots
freezing at gunshots, "seeing through floors", dead bots making callouts, two C4s planted in the same spot, a
NULL-entity crash, and defibrillator revives being let go too early. The path-finder was rewritten and the
per-tick world scans were cached, so a full server of bots costs noticeably less CPU than before.

[h2]Optional addons[/h2]
Extra behaviour switches on automatically when the matching addon is installed - Super Soda, Fake Soda, Boom
Body, Thomas the Tank Engine, the Minethrower, and the role addons behind the roles listed above. Nothing is
required beyond TTT2.
